Indian Cricketers

Rohit Sharma


1. Rohit Sharma is an Indian cricketer and current captain of the Indian national team in all formats.


2. He is known for his elegant batting style and holds the record for the highest individual score in ODI cricket (264 runs).


3. Nicknamed the "Hitman," he is famous for his ability to hit big sixes with ease.

Virat Kohli


1.Virat Kohli is one of the greatest modern-day batsmen, known for his consistency across all formats.


2.He has served as the captain of the Indian cricket team and holds numerous batting records.


3.Kohli is admired for his aggressive playing style and unmatched fitness levels.

MS Dhoni


1.MS Dhoni, also known as "Captain Cool," is a legendary former captain of the Indian cricket team.


2.He led India to major ICC trophies — T20 World Cup (2007), ODI World Cup (2011), and Champions Trophy (2013).


3.Dhoni is known for his calm demeanor, sharp wicketkeeping skills, and finishing matches under pressure.

Shreyas Iyer


1.Shreyas Iyer is a stylish middle-order batsman for India.


2.He has captained teams like Delhi Capitals and Kolkata Knight Riders in the IPL.


3.Known for his calm leadership and ability to handle pressure well.
